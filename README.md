# Shamil · شامل

> **Sort any messy folder, privately, on your own computer.**
> Shamil reads your documents, slides, sheets, and scans — in English and Arabic — and sorts them into clear groups. It runs entirely on your Mac or PC. Nothing is uploaded, ever.

## 🔒 Privacy & Safety
- **100% Offline**: All text extraction, OCR, clustering, and sorting run locally on your machine.
- **English & Arabic**: Native bilingual processing and OCR.
- **Zero Data Collected**: No telemetry, no cloud analysis, no accounts required.
- **Safe & Non-Destructive**: 1-click undo.
