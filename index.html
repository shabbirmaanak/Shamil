How it works

Three steps. About a minute.

  1. 1

    Choose a folder

    Downloads, Desktop, a USB stick, an external drive — any folder on your computer.

  2. 2

    Press Sort

  3. 3

    Look first. When you're happy, apply the groups as shortcut folders or move the files. Your folder is untouched until you say so.

Built for Arabic

Reads Arabic properly.

Most tools treat Arabic as an afterthought. Shamil folds letter variants (ه/ة, ي/ى, ك/ک), ignores harakat when matching and keeps your original spelling in group names — so الدعوة and الدعوه land in the same place.

  • Offline Arabic + English OCR built in
  • Optional per file — run OCR on just one scan
  • Ready for Lisān ud-Daʿwat (LSD) recognition models
  • Fix a misread word — your corrections are saved to train better models

Safety first

Your files are critical.
Shamil treats them that way.

  • Never deletes on its own. Duplicates are only suggested — you tick what to keep.
  • Uses your Trash / Recycle Bin. Anything you remove can be restored from there.
  • Shortcut folders by default. Your originals stay exactly where they are.
  • Full undo journal. Undo puts every file back and pulls removed copies out of the Trash — and tells you plainly about anything it couldn't restore.
  • Clear consent. A short disclaimer to tick before sorting or cleaning duplicates.

Private by design

No cloud. No AI service.
No account.

Shamil doesn't send your files, their text, or anything about them anywhere. The grouping, the OCR and the duplicate checks all run on your own processor. You can unplug from the internet and it works exactly the same.

Want to use it from the sofa? Open the address Shamil shows on your phone, type the PIN, and you're in — the connection stays inside your own Wi-Fi.

Install

Up and running in two minutes.

  1. Download and double-click it to unzip.
  2. Drag Shamil into your Applications folder.
  3. Open it. The first time, macOS says it "can't be opened" because Shamil isn't from the App Store yet. Click Done.
  4. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Shamil. You only do this once.
  5. Shamil opens in its own window. Choose a folder and press Sort.

FAQ

Questions people ask.

Is Shamil really free?

Yes. No account, no trial, no subscription.

Does it upload my files or use cloud AI?

No. Everything — reading, OCR, grouping and duplicate checks — happens on your own computer. Shamil works with the internet switched off.

Will it move or delete my files?

Only when you press Apply, and only in the way you choose. The default creates shortcut folders and leaves originals where they are. Duplicates are never removed without your tick, and removed copies go to the Trash / Recycle Bin.

Can I undo a sort days later?

Yes. Open the same folder in Shamil and it finds undo_sort.json. One click puts everything back, including files pulled out of the Trash.

Which file types does it read?

PDF (including scanned PDFs with OCR on), Word (.docx), PowerPoint (.pptx), Excel (.xlsx), text and CSV, and images (JPG, PNG and more) with OCR on.

How big a folder can it handle?

Thousands of files. Without OCR a few thousand files read in seconds; OCR takes longer and Shamil shows an estimate before you start.

Why does my Mac / Windows warn me when I open it?

Shamil isn't yet signed through Apple's or Microsoft's paid developer programmes, so the system shows a one-time warning. The install steps above show how to open it safely, and you can compare the checksum to make sure the file is genuine.

What does "Shamil" mean?

شامل (shāmil) is Arabic for comprehensive or all-inclusive — one place that takes in every file.
